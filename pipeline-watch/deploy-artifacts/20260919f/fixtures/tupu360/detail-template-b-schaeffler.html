Global Position Name

Project Office Coord.

Responsibilities

  • Support project management in organizing project-related processes, timelines, staff and resources.
  • Maintain project schedules, resources, equipment and information.
  • Monitor project progress and handle organizational issues.
  • Act as point of contact and communicate project status to all participants.
  • Maintain comprehensive project documentation, plans and reports.
  • Coordinate with different departments to ensure seamless project execution.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, Engineering, or a related field;
  • Open to fresh graduates and candidates with 1–3 years of relevant experience in project coordination, finance operations, process management, PMO, or related areas.
  • Good command of written and spoken English, with the ability to communicate in an international working environment.
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite, particularly PowerPoint, Excel, Word, and data visualization tools such as Power BI, as well as AI tools including deepseek and ChartGDP to support data-driven project insights.
  • Demonstrated strong organizational capabilities, with the ability to multitask, prioritize, and meet tight deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
  • Detail-oriented, responsible, proactive, and willing to learn, with the ability to work effectively with different departments and stakeholders.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively with a diverse set of stakeholders and departments, ensuring smooth communication and project coordination.
  • Understanding of agile methodologies and frameworks is a plus, contributing to flexible and adaptive project management practices.
